# 7. How to Export Output from the ADRF

To protect sensitive data, all output (tables, graphs, model output) must pass a formal **Export Review** before being removed from the ADRF.
- **Process Overview**: Review involves two stages: an initial Cursory Review and a final Comprehensive Review by the relevant Data Steward(s).
- **Timeline**: The review process targets completion within five business days, but delays can occur if reviewers require code/output changes.
- **Submission Requirements**: Only submit final, well-documented output files. Intermediate files will be rejected.
- **Confidentiality in Discussion**: Beyond the Export Review, remember that you may not discuss statistics in exact numbers (e.g., refer to percentages/proportions only within 25% ranges).

> 💡 You can find **detailed information** as well as **video tutorials** on how to submit export requests and retrieve approved exports in the **ADRF User Guide**, which is available to ADRF users in Secure PASS in their project workspaces.
